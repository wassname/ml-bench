# wassname-ml-bench

Twelve hard machine learning research problems from [wassname](https://wassname.org)'s own work,
none of them in any training set, each scored against his own answer. A score of 1.00 means the
model matched his answer, and the reference answer is not a ceiling.

The questions stay private, so this page shows the scores and the domains, and nothing that would
let a model be trained on them.
