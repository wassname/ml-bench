Every score is read through that seat's own two anchors, measured by `just calibrate` on fixed artifacts: `off-topic` is what it scores another item's reference answer, `gold` what it scores this item's own. So 0 and 1 mean the same on every seat, and a seat needs a `gap` above 0.5 to sit at all. `leniency` is what it adds in the middle of the scale, where the two anchors do not reach.

A seat never grades its own company, so those rows meet a different mixture. deepseek-v4-flash-0731 +0.005, deepseek-v4-flash-0731 (repeat:2) +0.005, deepseek-v4-flash-0731 (skill:ml-debug) +0.005, deepseek-v4-pro-0813 +0.005, gpt-5.6-luna -0.018, gpt-5.6-sol -0.018, gpt-5.6-terra -0.018, gpt-oss-120b -0.018, inkling +0.005, inkling-small +0.005 is what that costs them, against a full panel.
